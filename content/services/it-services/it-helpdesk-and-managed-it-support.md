## Overview

In a growing company, IT usually lands on whoever is good with computers. That works until they go on leave, or leave.

We give you a proper helpdesk, with people who know your setup and write things down.

## What's covered

- One point of contact by phone, email, chat and a ticket portal
- Account creation, access changes and password resets
- Joiner and leaver checklists, tied to our HR onboarding
- Device management, software installation, patching and antivirus
- Backup monitoring and restore tests
- Coordination with internet, hardware and software vendors

## How requests work

Every request becomes a ticket with a priority and a response time agreed in writing. You can see what's open and what's done. Once a month you get a short report on volumes, response times and anything that keeps coming back.

## Overview

Testing is the step teams skip when they're running late, and the one they regret. We can test as part of a build or as a separate service.

## What's included

- Test planning and test cases
- Functional and regression testing
- Performance and load testing
- Mobile device and browser coverage
- Clear bug reports your developers can act on
- Re-testing after fixes

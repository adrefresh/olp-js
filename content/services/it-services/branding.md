## Overview

A brand is more than a logo. It's how you describe yourself, how you look everywhere, and what people expect when they deal with you.

## What's included

- Brand strategy: audience, positioning and what makes you different
- Naming and tagline
- Logo and visual identity
- Brand guidelines
- Messaging and tone of voice
- Stationery, presentation and document templates
- Website and social media look and feel
- A launch plan, inside and outside the company

## Steps

### Discover

Positioning and messaging.

### Design

Identity and guidelines.

### Launch

Rolled out everywhere at once.

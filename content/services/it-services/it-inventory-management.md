## Overview

Most companies find out what they own when something goes missing. Then comes the scramble: who had that laptop, is it still under warranty, why are we paying for twenty licences when twelve people use them?

## What's included

- Asset tagging and a central register
- Assignment to named employees, and returns when they leave
- Warranty and support contract tracking with reminders
- Software licence tracking
- Regular physical audits across offices and remote staff
- Reports finance can use for depreciation and audits
- Refresh planning, so you replace devices before they become a problem

## Steps

### Audit

We count what's actually there.

### Register

Every item tagged and recorded.

### Keep current

Joiners, leavers and repairs update the register.

## FAQ

### Can you take over our existing records?

Yes. We check them against a physical audit and fix the gaps.

### What does the register include?

Every device and licence: who has it, where it is, cost, warranty dates and condition.

### Do you track software licences too?

Yes. We record what you've bought, who's using it and when it renews.

### Can finance use the reports?

Yes. We provide reports for depreciation and audits.

### How often do you audit?

We agree a schedule with you, usually once or twice a year, plus spot checks.

### Do you track remote employees' devices?

Yes, including where they are and who has them.

## Overview

Nothing says "we weren't ready" like a new hire spending their first morning without a laptop. We take that off your list and tie it to your hiring.

## What we do

- Order to your specification
- Install the operating system, software and security settings
- Ship to the office or the employee's home
- Hand over with a short guide
- Collect, wipe and redeploy when someone leaves
- Record everything in your asset register

## Steps

### Tell us the start date

And the role.

### We prepare

Ordered, set up and tested.

### Delivered

Before they start.

## FAQ

### How early do you need to know about a new hire?

The earlier, the better. We agree a lead time that suits the devices you use.

### Can you ship to home addresses?

Yes, across India, with tracking.

### What about devices from people who leave?

We collect them, wipe them securely and put them back into stock.

### Can you pre-install our software?

Yes. We set up devices to your standard image.

### Does it work with Employer of Record hires?

Yes. The laptop is ordered with the offer, so it arrives before the start date.

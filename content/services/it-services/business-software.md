## Overview

Not every company needs a big system. Many need accounts, payroll, customer records and tickets that talk to each other. We help you pick the tools and make them work.

- **Sales** — Zoho CRM
- **HR and payroll** — Keka, Superworks and Zoho People
- **Ticketing** — Zoho Desk, Freshdesk and Jira Service Management

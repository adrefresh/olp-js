## Overview

For a project team, a short engagement or a company that would rather keep cash for other things, renting makes more sense than buying.

## What's included

- Laptops and desktops to your specification
- Set-up and delivery
- Support and replacement if something fails
- Add or return devices as the team changes
- Collection and secure data wiping at the end

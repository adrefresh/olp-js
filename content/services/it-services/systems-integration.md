## Overview

Most businesses end up with tools that don't talk to each other: tickets in one, customers in another, payroll in a third. People copy data across by hand and mistakes follow. We connect the systems and set them up so they keep working.

## What we connect

- Ticketing and email, so requests become tickets and replies go back
- CRM, quotes and accounting, so a won deal becomes an invoice in Zoho Books or Tally
- HRMS, attendance and payroll, without re-typing
- User accounts and single sign-on, so joiners get access and leavers lose it in one step
- Chat alerts in Teams, Slack or Zoho Cliq
- Dashboards that pull from all of them

## For IT and software companies

- Support desk (Zoho Desk, Freshdesk or Jira Service Management) linked to CRM and engineering tools
- Project and timesheet tools feeding billing and payroll
- Keka or Superworks connected to attendance, leave and payroll
- CRM linked to invoicing and revenue reports

## For manufacturing companies

- ERP linked to accounting, inventory, purchasing and production planning
- Barcode and QR scanning on stock and dispatch
- Biometric and shift attendance into payroll
- GST e-invoicing and e-way bills from the accounting system
- Quality and maintenance records linked to production
- Supplier and dealer portals tied to orders and stock

## How we choose

If a vendor's own integration works, we use it. If it doesn't, we build a small connector with APIs or a tool like Zoho Flow, and document it so someone else can maintain it.

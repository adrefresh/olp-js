## Overview

The tools are easy to buy and surprisingly easy to set up badly: open sharing, no multi-factor sign-in, a mess of folders nobody understands. We set them up so they stay tidy.

## What's included

- Tenant setup and domain and email configuration
- User onboarding and offboarding
- Multi-factor sign-in and security settings
- Teams, SharePoint, Drive and shared mailboxes
- Migrations from another email or file system
- Short training sessions for your team

## Overview

Most software problems are design problems that were found too late. Testing a clickable prototype with real users costs far less than rebuilding.

## What's included

- User research and journey mapping
- Wireframes and clickable prototypes
- Visual design and design systems
- Usability testing
- Handover to development with specifications

## Steps

### Research

Who uses it and how.

### Prototype

Test before building.

### Hand over

Clear specs for developers.

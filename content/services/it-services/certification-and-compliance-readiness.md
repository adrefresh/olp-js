## Overview

Customers abroad increasingly ask Indian suppliers to show they handle data and processes properly. The certificate is issued by an independent body. We do the groundwork.

## Standards we prepare you for

- **ISO 27001** — information security
- **SOC 2 Type I and II** — security and availability controls
- **ISO 27701** — privacy information management
- **ISO 20000-1** — IT service management
- **ISO 9001** — quality management

## The route

A gap assessment, policies and a risk register, technical controls, staff training, evidence collection, an internal audit before the real one, coordination with the auditor, and upkeep through the surveillance audits that follow.

## Steps

### Assess

Where you are against the standard.

### Build

Policies, controls and evidence.

### Audit

We manage it with the certifying body.

### Maintain

Through yearly reviews.

## FAQ

### Do you issue the certificate?

No. An independent certifying body does. We prepare you and manage the process.

### How long does it take?

It depends on the standard and where you start. We give a realistic range after the gap assessment.

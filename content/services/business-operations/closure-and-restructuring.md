## Overview

Not every story is about starting up. Sometimes two entities become one, a company changes structure, or a business has run its course. Done properly, it's a process. Done badly, it leaves loose ends that come back years later.

## What we help with

- Mergers and amalgamations
- Converting one type of entity into another
- Changing structure as the business changes
- Winding up or striking off a company
- Handling employees and contracts during the change
- Final filings, so nothing is left open

## Why it matters

Indian compliance isn't one big thing. It's dozens of small deadlines under different laws, and a missed one rarely hurts on the day. It turns up months later as a late fee, a notice, or a question from an investor's lawyer.

Most companies we meet aren't careless. Nobody's job is to watch the calendar.

## What we look after

- Annual filings with the Registrar of Companies, board and shareholder meetings, statutory registers
- Director KYC and other periodic filings
- GST registration and returns
- PF, ESI and Professional Tax payments and returns
- Shops and Establishment and other labour law requirements
- FEMA and RBI reporting for foreign-owned companies
- Policy reviews and audit preparation

## We start with a health check

Before we take over, we go through what has been filed and what hasn't. If there are gaps, you get a list in plain language, ranked by risk, with a sensible order to clear them. No lectures.

## Who this suits

Companies with an Indian entity and nobody in-house who owns compliance, and overseas parents who want someone local watching the deadlines.

## FAQ

### What if we've missed filings?

Common, and fixable. We list what's overdue, rank it by risk and clear it in order.

### Who signs the filings?

Directors and authorised signatories still sign what the law requires. We prepare everything and chase signatures.

### How do we know what's due?

You get one dated calendar and a short monthly note on what was filed and what's next.

### Do you cover foreign-owned companies?

Yes, including the reporting that comes with foreign investment under FEMA and RBI rules.

### Can you work with our existing accountant?

Yes. We coordinate with them so nothing is filed twice or missed.

### Do you handle GST and income tax?

GST returns are part of compliance. Bookkeeping and income tax sit under Accounting, Tax and Audit.

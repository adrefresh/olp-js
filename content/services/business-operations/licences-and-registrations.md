## Overview

Which licences you need depends on what you actually do, where, and with how many people. We work that out first, then handle the applications.

## What's included

- A review of the registrations and approvals your activities require
- Sector-specific approvals
- Import-export code
- Labour and establishment registrations
- Renewals tracked on your compliance calendar

## Steps

### Review

What you do and where.

### Apply

Documents, forms and follow-up.

### Track

Renewals added to the calendar.

## FAQ

### How do I know which licences I need?

We review your activities and tell you, in writing.

### What if you don't hold the licence yourselves?

We tell you who does, and manage the process around it.

## Overview

Somebody always ends up chasing the courier, renewing the insurance and finding last year's agreement. It's rarely the person you hired to do something else. We take as much of it as you want.

## What we can take on

- Document and records management, including statutory registers and contracts
- Vendor onboarding, agreements and renewals
- Office administration: office supplies and furniture sourcing, utilities, courier and facilities coordination
- Travel and event logistics
- Insurance and policy renewals
- Licence and subscription tracking
- Operational support for founders and leadership

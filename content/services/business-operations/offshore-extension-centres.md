## Overview

Many overseas companies want people in India without setting up and running a whole business there. We build that for you, in stages, so you only take on what you need.

## Three ways to start

- **Employer of Record** — Start hiring now, with no entity.
- **Subsidiary** — Your own Indian company, once the team justifies it.
- **Offshore centre** — A larger team for a function like engineering, support or finance operations.

## What we set up

Entity, workspace, hiring, payroll, laptops and compliance, planned together. We'll also tell you honestly when staying on Employer of Record a while longer is the better choice.

## Steps

### Plan

Roles, timeline and the right structure.

### Start small

First hires, a desk and payroll.

### Scale

More people, a bigger space, your own entity if it makes sense.

### Move across

Transfer the team to your entity without a gap.

## FAQ

### Is this the same as outsourcing?

No. These are your people, doing your work, under your direction. We handle the legal, payroll and operating setup around them.

### Do we need our own company from the start?

Not necessarily. Many clients begin on Employer of Record and move to their own subsidiary once the team has grown.

### How quickly can a team be set up?

It depends on the size and the roles. We give you a realistic plan, including hiring time, before you commit.

### Who manages the team day to day?

You do, or the leaders you appoint. Our HR Business Partner service can support them if you'd like.

### How is our confidential information protected?

Employment agreements include confidentiality and IP assignment from day one, and our IT team can set up secure access to your systems.

### How many people before an entity makes sense?

There's no single number. It depends on cost, control and plans. We'll model it with you.

### Can we move people from EOR to our entity?

Yes, with their service history carried over.

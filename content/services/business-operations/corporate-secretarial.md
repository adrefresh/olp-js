## Overview

A share allotment without the right resolution, or a director change that was never filed, can cause trouble years later, often at the worst moment, such as an investment round.

## What's included

- Board and shareholder meeting notices, agendas and minutes
- Resolutions, including written ones
- Share allotments and transfers
- Changes to directors, registered office and authorised capital
- Statutory registers kept up to date
- Annual general meeting arrangements

## FAQ

### How many board meetings do we need?

The law sets minimums that vary by type of company. We'll tell you what applies to yours and plan the year.

### Can you help before a funding round?

Yes. We tidy the records first, so due diligence doesn't turn up surprises.

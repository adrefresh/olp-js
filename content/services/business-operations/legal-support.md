## Overview

Most business legal work is routine and easy to get wrong: a vendor contract with the wrong liability clause, an employment agreement that doesn't hold up when it's needed.

## What's covered

- Client, vendor and partnership agreements
- NDAs and confidentiality terms
- Employment agreements, offer letters and HR policies
- Shareholder and founder agreements
- Notices to and replies from authorities
- Legal checks on compliance matters
- Disputes and court matters, through our partner lawyers

## Steps

### Tell us

Send the document or describe the issue.

### We brief the lawyer

With the context they need, so you don't repeat yourself.

### You get a clear answer

Risks flagged in plain language, with a recommendation.

### We follow up

Until it's signed, filed or settled.

## FAQ

### Are you a law firm?

No. The legal work is done by our partner lawyers, and we coordinate it so you have one contact.

### Can you review a contract we've been sent?

Yes. Tell us when you need it back, and we'll flag the risks in plain language.

### Can you draft employment agreements?

Yes, along with offer letters and HR policies, checked against Indian law.

### Do you handle disputes?

Our partner lawyers do, and we manage the process for you.

### Do you cover legal matters outside India?

No. Our legal support is for Indian law and Indian businesses.

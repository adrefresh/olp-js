## Overview

Most small and mid-sized companies don't need a finance department. They need clean books every month, filings that aren't late, and someone who can answer the auditor's questions.

## What's included

- Bookkeeping, payables and receivables
- Monthly closing and management reports
- GST registration and returns
- TDS and income tax filings, and advance tax planning
- Payroll tax coordination with our payroll team
- Support through statutory and tax audits
- Replies to tax notices
- Reporting packs for an overseas parent

## Steps

### Review

We look at your books, filings and software.

### Set up

Routines, cut-off dates and a chart of accounts that makes sense.

### Close each month

Books closed, reports sent, filings made.

### Year end

Audit preparation and queries handled with your auditor.

## FAQ

### Can you work with our own accountant?

Yes. We can do the monthly work and leave planning to them, or the other way round.

### Which software do you use?

Zoho Books, Tally, or whatever you already run.

### Do you carry out the audit?

No. The statutory audit is done by an independent chartered accountant. We prepare the records and handle their queries.

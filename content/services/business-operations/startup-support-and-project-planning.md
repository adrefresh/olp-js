## Overview

Decisions made in the first year, about who owns what, how people are hired and what gets filed, are the ones that cost most to unpick later, usually in the middle of a funding round.

## What's included

- A clean cap table and share records
- Investor-ready company records
- An operating structure that fits your plans
- A first-year plan covering setup, hiring and compliance
- A realistic budget for the basics

## Steps

### Talk

Where you are and where you're heading.

### Plan

A written roadmap for the first year.

### Do

We carry out the parts you want help with.

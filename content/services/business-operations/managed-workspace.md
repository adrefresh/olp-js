## Overview

Taking your own office in India means a lease, a deposit, interiors, internet, a landlord and someone to call when the AC fails. For a team of five, that's a lot of effort for something that isn't your business.

With us you move into space that's already set up and pay for the seats you use.

## What's included

- Furnished seats, from a small team up to a dedicated floor
- Power, high-speed internet and backup
- Meeting rooms and common areas
- Housekeeping, security and pantry
- IT support and equipment from our IT team
- A registered address, where your set-up allows it
- Flexible terms, so you can add seats as the team grows

## One provider for the whole team

Say you're an overseas company hiring five people in India. We can employ them through Employer of Record, run payroll, deliver their laptops and seat them in the same office. One agreement, one point of contact.

## FAQ

### Can we add seats later?

Yes. That's the point of flexible terms. We'll agree notice periods upfront.

### Can we use the address to register our company?

Often, depending on the agreement and your entity type. We confirm before you sign.

### Do you manage offices we already lease?

No. Our service is the workspace we provide.

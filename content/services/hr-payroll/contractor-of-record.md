## Overview

If someone works full time, under your direction, for your business alone, a tax authority may not see them as an independent contractor at all. That's the risk. We deal with it.

## What's included

- Contractor agreements with clear scope, IP and confidentiality terms
- Identity, tax and bank verification
- A review of whether the arrangement is really a contractor relationship
- Invoice collection, checking and approval
- Payment in the contractor's currency
- Records you can hand to an auditor

## Steps

### Review

We check the arrangement.

### Contract

Agreements and verification.

### Invoice

Submitted, checked and sent for your approval.

### Pay

One payment from you, one to them.

## FAQ

### What does a Contractor of Record do?

We contract with the individual, verify their documents, handle invoices and pay them. You deal with one point of contact.

### How do we know the person is really a contractor?

We review the arrangement, looking at how they work, who directs them and how much of their time is yours, and tell you if it looks like employment.

### What if they should be an employee?

We can move them onto Employer of Record without a break in the work.

### Who owns the work they produce?

You do. The contractor agreement assigns the work to your company.

### What records do you keep?

Agreements, verified documents, invoices and payments, in a form you can hand to an auditor.

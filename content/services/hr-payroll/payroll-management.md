## Overview

Payroll is the one job that has to be right every single month. Nobody notices when it's fine. Everybody notices when it's late or wrong.

We take over the whole cycle, whether you have your own Indian company or hire through our Employer of Record service.

## A month with us

You send the changes: joiners, exits, leave, bonuses, reimbursements. We calculate, you review and approve, we pay your team and deposit what's due. Payslips go out, and your accountant gets the journal.

## What's covered

- Salary structure design, including how CTC splits into components
- Attendance and leave data
- Provident fund, ESI, professional tax and labour welfare fund
- TDS on salaries, investment declarations and Form 16
- Reimbursements, bonuses, arrears and off-cycle runs
- Salary payment and statutory challans
- Full and final settlements
- Payroll journals and monthly reports

## Taking over mid-year

Moving payroll in the middle of a financial year is normal. We collect year-to-date figures so tax and statutory records carry on correctly.

## Who this suits

Companies with an Indian entity who want to hand payroll over, and anyone whose payroll is currently a spreadsheet and one very tired HR person.

## Steps

### Share your data

Employee details, salary structures and last year's records.

### Test run

We can run the first month alongside your current process, so the numbers can be compared.

### Go live

We take over, with a cut-off date and a reminder before it.

### Every month

Payment, filings, payslips and a short report.

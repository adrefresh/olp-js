## Overview

A policy nobody has read is no help when something goes wrong. Ours are short, written for the people who have to follow them, and checked against the rules that apply to your location.

## What's covered

- Leave and attendance
- Remote and flexible working
- Code of conduct
- Grievance handling
- Prevention of sexual harassment (POSH)
- Employee handbook
- Rollout, so people actually read them

## Steps

### Review

What you have.

### Write

Plain language, legally checked.

### Roll out

Short briefings and acknowledgements.

## FAQ

### Can you update our existing handbook?

Yes. We keep what works.

You do. We update them when the rules change, if you'd like.

## Overview

Nothing slows a first week like waiting for equipment. We tie it to hiring, so it's ordered with the offer and arrives before the start date.

## What's included

- Devices ordered to your specification
- Set-up with software and security settings
- Delivery to the office or the employee's home
- Collection and data wiping when someone leaves
- Every item recorded in your asset register

## Steps

### Tell us the start date

And the role.

### We prepare

Ordered and configured.

### Delivered

Before day one.

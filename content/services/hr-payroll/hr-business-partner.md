## Two ways to work

- **Shared** — A senior HR professional supporting a few companies, with set hours each week. A good fit for smaller teams.
- **Dedicated** — One person working only for you, inside your team, in your meetings, knowing your people by name.

## What they do

- Write and maintain HR policies and the handbook
- Manage the employee lifecycle, from offer to exit
- Run appraisal cycles
- Advise managers on difficult conversations
- Handle employee relations and documentation
- Support engagement and retention
- Keep you aligned with labour laws as they change

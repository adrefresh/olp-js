## How it differs

Hire Talent finds permanent employees for your payroll. Employer of Record employs people you've chosen, usually long term. Contract Staffing supplies people for a fixed period or project, employed and paid by us, managed day to day by your team.

## What's included

- Sourcing and screening against your brief
- Fixed-term contracts with clear start and end dates
- Payroll, statutory contributions and insurance
- Compliance that comes with contract labour, where it applies
- Quick scale-up and scale-down
- Replacement if someone leaves mid-contract <span class='ph'>confirm</span>
- The option to convert a contractor to permanent

## Steps

### Brief

Roles, numbers, duration.

### Source

We shortlist and you choose.

### Deploy

People start, with contracts and onboarding done.

### Review

Extend, end or convert.

## FAQ

### Who manages the contract staff?

Your team manages the work. We manage employment, payroll and compliance.

### Can we hire them permanently later?

Yes. We'll explain the process and any conditions upfront.

## Overview

Hiring someone is the easy part. People rarely leave because of one bad day. They drift, and by the time the resignation arrives it's too late to change anything.

Our engagement service gives you a steady read on how your team feels and a programme that responds to it. It works on its own, or alongside our HR Business Partner and Employer of Record services.

## What we run

- **Engagement and pulse surveys** — Short, anonymous, reported in groups, with a one-page summary of what to act on.
- **Recognition and rewards** — Simple programmes managers will actually use, from peer shout-outs to milestone awards.
- **Wellness** — Health camps, wellness sessions and access to counselling.
- **Team events** — Offsites, festival celebrations, team lunches and learning days, planned and managed by us.
- **Onboarding experience** — Welcome kits, buddy programmes and check-ins at 30, 60 and 90 days.
- **Stay and exit interviews** — Honest conversations about why people stay and why they go.
- **Internal communication** — Newsletters, town halls and announcements people read.

## For remote and overseas teams

If your India team has never met your leadership, engagement matters even more. We help them feel part of your company, not a name on a vendor's payroll list, with regular check-ins, shared events and a clear line of communication back to you.

## How we measure it

Participation, engagement scores over time, retention, and the reasons people give when they leave. You get a short report each quarter with what's changed and what we suggest doing next.

## Who this is for

Growing companies without a dedicated people team, overseas companies with an India team, and HR leads who want help running programmes, not just designing them.

## Steps

### Listen

A short survey and a conversation with your leadership.

### Plan

A year-long calendar of programmes, with a budget you agree upfront.

### Run

Our team runs the surveys, events and recognition, so your managers don't have to.

### Measure

Quarterly reports on scores, retention and exit reasons.

### Adjust

We change the plan based on what the numbers and the conversations tell us.

## FAQ

### Do you run the activities yourselves?

Yes. Our own team plans and runs them. You don't need to coordinate vendors.

### Can we start with just surveys?

Yes. Many clients start there and add events and recognition later.

### Are survey responses confidential?

Results are reported in groups and never traced back to individuals.

### Does it work for EOR employees?

Yes. We include them in the same programmes as your other India team members.

## Onboarding

- Offer letters and joining documents
- Background checks
- Equipment arranged through our IT team
- System access and accounts
- Orientation plan and first-week schedule
- Check-ins at 30, 60 and 90 days

## Offboarding

- Handling resignations and notice periods
- Handover plan
- Access removal and device return
- Full and final settlement
- Experience and relieving letters
- Exit interview

## Steps

### Tell us the date

Joining or last day.

### We run the checklist

Nothing missed.

### You get a summary

Everything done and recorded.

## FAQ

### What does onboarding include?

Offer letters, documents, background checks, equipment, accounts, orientation, and check-ins at 30, 60 and 90 days.

### What does offboarding include?

Notice handling, handover, access removal, device return, final settlement, letters and an exit interview.

### Can you do offboarding only?

Yes.

### Does it work with Employer of Record hires?

Yes. It's built into that service.

### Who does the paperwork?

We do. You approve, and we handle the rest.

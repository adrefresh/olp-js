## Overview

The right benefits are the ones people actually use. We start with who is in your team and what they'd value, then build around your budget.

## What's included

- Group health insurance
- Accident cover
- Wellness, meal and travel allowances
- Enrolment of new joiners and removal of leavers
- Claims support
- Renewals and reviews

## Steps

Team and budget.

### Choose

Options compared side by side.

### Run

Enrolment, claims and renewals.

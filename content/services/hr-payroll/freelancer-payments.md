## Overview

Paying freelancers is where many finance teams lose patience: different currencies, bank rules, missing documents and invoices in every format.

## How it works

- **Onboard** — The freelancer is invited, verified and signs the agreement.
- **Collect** — We gather tax and bank details once and keep them current.
- **Invoice** — Standard format, routed to you for approval.
- **Pay** — You make one payment. We pay each freelancer and issue the right paperwork.
- **Report** — A monthly summary and year-end records.

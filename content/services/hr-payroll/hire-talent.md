## Overview

Good hiring starts with a few honest questions: what will this person actually do in their first six months, which experience really matters, and which lines on the job description are just nice to have. We ask them before we search.

## How we work with you

- Role scoping and job description support
- Sourcing across job boards, networks and direct outreach
- Screening and skills assessment
- Interview coordination and feedback
- Salary benchmarking and offer negotiation
- Reference and background checks
- Onboarding support after the offer is accepted

## Roles we cover

Technology, finance, operations, sales, marketing and support, from individual contributors to team leads. If you're building a whole team, we assign a dedicated recruiter and run it like a project.

## Hire and employ in one place

If you'd like us to employ the people we place, they move straight onto our Employer of Record service. One provider from search to first payslip.

## Steps

### Brief

A call to understand the role, the team and the budget.

### Search

We source and screen against what really matters.

### Shortlist

A few people, with our notes on each.

### Offer

We help with negotiation and checks.

### Start

Onboarding, equipment and, if you want it, payroll.

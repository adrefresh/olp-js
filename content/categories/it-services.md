## Overview

Most IT problems aren't dramatic. A password reset that takes two days. A server that was supposed to be migrated in March. A new hire who starts Monday and has no laptop. We deal with those, and with the bigger projects behind them: device rollouts for new teams, directory services, application builds.

## FAQ

### Can you supply devices to remote employees?

Yes. We order, configure and deliver to the office or the employee's home, and collect and wipe the device when they leave.

### What happens to our code if we stop?

You own it. We hand over source code, documentation and access.

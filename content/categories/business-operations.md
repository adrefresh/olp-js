## Overview

The paperwork around a business is dull until it goes wrong. A missed filing turns into a penalty. A badly chosen structure turns into a tax problem two years later. We help you pick the right structure at the start, get every registration done in the right order, and keep an eye on deadlines so you don't have to.

## FAQ

### How long does incorporation in India take?

Usually a few weeks from the point we have all your documents. Foreign-owned companies take longer, mostly because of bank accounts and KYC.

### Do I need to be in India?

No. Much of it can be handled remotely.
